# Module 3: Lecture Content Creation & Academic Workflows

Use these prompts to prepare bilingual explanations, lecture handouts, and literature summaries.

## 1. Multi-Level Concept Explainer
**Prompt to Copy:**
> "Explain the concept of **[Insert Complex Topic]** in three distinct ways for my college lecture:
> 1. A simple everyday analogy suitable for first-year students struggling with the basics.
> 2. A rigorous academic definition with key terminology for exam preparation.
> 3. Three common student misconceptions about this topic and how to clarify them in class."

## 2. Bilingual Classroom Support (English + Regional Language)
**Prompt to Copy:**
> "Translate the following key lecture summary into clear, academic **[Insert Language, e.g., Hindi / Marathi / Tamil / Bengali]** while keeping standard technical terms in English in brackets so students from regional-medium backgrounds can follow easily: **[Paste Summary Text]**."

## 3. Research Paper Synthesis for Faculty
**Prompt to Copy:**
> "I am pasting the abstract and conclusion of a recent research paper below. Summarize:
> - The core research gap addressed
> - The methodology used
> - Key findings and how I can mention this study as a modern example in my **[Insert Subject]** lecture.
> 
> Text: **[Paste Paper Text]**"
