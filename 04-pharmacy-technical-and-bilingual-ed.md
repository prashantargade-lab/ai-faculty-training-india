# Module 4: Pharmacy, Technical Education & Bilingual Pedagogy (English–Marathi)

Specialized Claude prompt templates for Pharmacy, Engineering/Technical faculty, and bilingual classroom support under NEP 2020.

## 1. Pharmacy & Health Sciences: Clinical Case & Drug Mechanism Builder
**Prompt to Copy:**
> "Act as a senior Pharmacy professor teaching **[Insert Subject, e.g., Pharmacology / Pharmaceutics / Pharmaceutical Chemistry]** for B.Pharm Semester **[X]** students (PCI syllabus aligned). 
> 
> For the drug/formulation **[Insert Drug or Topic]**, generate:
> - A concise mechanism of action and key pharmacokinetic parameters
> - One realistic Indian clinical or patient-counseling case scenario for classroom discussion
> - Three viva-voce questions with ideal answers to test practical understanding."

## 2. Technical & Engineering Education: Lab Manual & Viva Design
**Prompt to Copy:**
> "Design a structured laboratory experiment sheet for **[Insert Practical/Lab Title]** for undergraduate technical students. Include:
> - Aim, required apparatus/software, and safety precautions
> - Step-by-step experimental procedure
> - A troubleshooting table listing 3 common student errors and how to fix them
> - 5 conceptual viva questions focusing on 'why' rather than memorization."

## 3. English–Marathi Bilingual Concept Bridge (NEP 2020 Support)
**Prompt to Copy:**
> "I am teaching **[Insert Technical or Pharmacy Topic]** to undergraduate students in Maharashtra who transition from Marathi-medium schooling to English-medium higher education.
> 
> Create a bilingual study note that:
> - Explains the core concept clearly in simple **Marathi**, while retaining standard scientific and technical terms in **English** (e.g., *Bioavailability (जैवउपलब्धता)*)
> - Provides a 5-word English-to-Marathi technical glossary for the topic
> - Includes 3 short English sentence starters students can use to write accurate answers in university exams."

## Hands-On Faculty Exercise (20 Minutes)
1. **Pharmacy & Technical Faculty:** Select one practical experiment or drug monograph from your current syllabus and generate a viva-voce + troubleshooting guide using Prompt 1 or 2.
2. **Bilingual Pedagogy Practice:** Take a complex paragraph from an English textbook and run Prompt 3 to create a student-friendly Marathi–English bridge handout.
3. **Peer Review:** Share the generated output with a colleague at your table to verify technical accuracy and clarity.
