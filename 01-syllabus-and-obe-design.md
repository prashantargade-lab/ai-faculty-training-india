# Module 1: Syllabus & Outcome-Based Education (OBE) Design

Use these prompts in Claude to align college courses with Bloom's Taxonomy and NEP/OBE frameworks.

## 1. Generating Course Outcomes (COs)
**Prompt to Copy:**
> "Act as a senior university curriculum designer in India. I am teaching an undergraduate course on **[Insert Subject Name]** for **[Insert Semester/Year, e.g., B.Sc. Semester 3]** students. 
> 
> Create 5 measurable Course Outcomes (CO1 to CO5) aligned with Bloom's Taxonomy (spanning Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, and Evaluate). Use clear action verbs, and present the output in a clean table mapping each CO to its Bloom's level."

## 2. Unit-Wise Lesson Plan Generator
**Prompt to Copy:**
> "Based on the following syllabus unit: **[Paste Unit Syllabus Text]**, design a 6-hour lecture plan divided into six 1-hour sessions. 
> 
> For each session, include:
> - Core topic and learning objective
> - One real-world Indian industry or societal example
> - One 5-minute active learning activity to engage a class of 60 students."

## Hands-On Faculty Exercise (15 Minutes)
1. Paste one unit from your current semester syllabus into Claude.
2. Run the **Unit-Wise Lesson Plan Generator** prompt above.
3. Ask a follow-up prompt: *"Suggest two low-cost classroom demonstrations or case studies that require zero lab equipment."*
