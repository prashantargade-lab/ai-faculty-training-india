# Claude Toolkit for Higher & Technical Education Faculty

An open-access collection of prompt frameworks, hands-on workshop exercises, and bilingual (English–Marathi) teaching templates created for Pharmacy, Engineering, and Higher Education faculty in India.

## Workshop Modules
1. **[Module 1: Syllabus & Outcome-Based Education (OBE) Design](./01-syllabus-and-obe-design.md)** – Aligning courses with Bloom's Taxonomy and creating session plans.
2. **[Module 2: Assessments, Question Banks & Grading Rubrics](./02-assessments-and-rubrics.md)** – Designing case-based questions and transparent evaluation rubrics.
3. **[Module 3: Lecture Content Creation & Academic Workflows](./03-teaching-content-and-research.md)** – Creating analogies, bilingual study aids, and research summaries.
4. **[Module 4: Pharmacy, Technical Education & Bilingual Pedagogy](./04-pharmacy-technical-and-bilingual-ed.md)** – PCI-aligned pharmacy cases, technical lab viva guides, and English–Marathi concept bridges.

## About This Repository
Created for Faculty Development Programs (FDPs) and hands-on AI training workshops across higher and technical education institutions. Educators are welcome to fork, adapt, and share these prompts freely.
