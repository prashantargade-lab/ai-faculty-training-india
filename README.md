# Claude Toolkit for Higher-Education Faculty

An open-access collection of prompt frameworks, hands-on workshop exercises, and teaching templates created for college and university educators in India.

## What Is Inside
- **Syllabus & Lesson Design:** Prompts for structuring outcome-based education (OBE) modules and course plans.
- **Assessment & Rubrics:** Templates for creating grading rubrics, case studies, and Bloom's Taxonomy quiz questions.
- **Hands-On Workshop Exercises:** Step-by-step lab activities used during Faculty Development Programs (FDPs).

## How to Use
Educators and trainers are welcome to copy, adapt, and share these prompts for classroom teaching and faculty workshops.
